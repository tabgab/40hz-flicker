40 Hz Flicker

Full-screen black/white flicker, locked to the display's refresh.

Photosensitivity warning

Flashing light can cause seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy, including people who have never had a seizure. Do not use this if you or anyone who can see the screen has epilepsy or a history of seizures. Stop at once if you feel dizzy, get a headache, see altered vision, or feel unwell.

This app is not a medical device. Ask a doctor before you use 40 Hz light or sound as a therapy.
